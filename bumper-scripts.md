# Starlight Radio 84.5 FM — bumper scripts

Station: **84.5 FM Starlight Radio**, the official station of the Starlight Arcade.
Voice: dry, warm, slightly deadpan. Two formats:

- **Station ID** (4–6 s): one line, ends or opens with the call sign.
- **Bit** (8–12 s): a small observation about arcade life, optional call-sign sting at the end.
